ELEVATE VISUAL IDENTITY / PROPOSED v0.8 / 13 OCTOBER 2026

This package is a design proposal. It is not a production or trademark approval.
20 SVGs: horizontal, stacked, wordmark, symbol and micro, each in teal, orange,
ink and white. SVGs contain outlined paths, with no linked font dependency.

The ELEVATE wordmark is custom-drawn lettering, not a complete typeface.
The descriptor is outlined DM Sans. DM Sans font and SIL OFL 1.1 license are
included. Keep the copyright and license with redistributed font software.
Do not claim exclusive rights to DM Sans. Trademark/name rights remain separate.

White #FFFFFF / Deep teal #087F80 / Warm orange #F39A3B / Ink #173536.
Use white on teal, ink on white, or ink on orange for ordinary text.
Do not use white on orange for readable copy. No gradients or added effects.

Keep proportions and original paths. Clear space: at least half the wordmark
cap height around full lockups, or one quarter symbol height around symbols.
Use the micro icon for small applications. Confirm actual size with a proof.
Physical production requires vendor samples and brand approval.

Mockups on the guide illustrate direction, not available merchandise or
verified store installations. Do not use AI mockups as master logo artwork.
Do not replace signs, order stock or change live brand assets without approval.
